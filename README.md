The World in One Day — Thursday 26 November 2026. A program that paints a broadsheet front page summing up the day's world news in a single picture: a navy masthead, one wide lead story, six smaller stories in a two-by-three grid, and a Thanksgiving footer. Everything is drawn in code with only the Python standard library — the PNG writer, the bitmap alphabet and every pictogram are defined in the program — so the picture is byte-for-byte identical on every run. Output: `entry.png` (1024×1024) in `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.

## The stories

- **Lead — Trade: US proposes tariffs on 14 partners.** New tariffs on 14 major trading partners to fight excess factory capacity, the highest rates aimed at China.
- **1. China — Xi graft probe hits Shanghai chief.** An anti-graft investigation into Chen Jining upends the usual path to power.
- **2. Space — Pakistani astronaut boards Tiangong.** The first foreigner to board China's space station, flying on Shenzhou-24.
- **3. Courts — Huawei convicted in US trial.** A New York jury finds Huawei guilty of stealing US trade secrets.
- **4. Markets — Anthropic slides below IPO price.** Shares fall under the $83 IPO price days after the listing.
- **5. Gulf — Houthis claim Saudi refinery hit.** Brent crude tops $94 a barrel.
- **6. Colombia — Police free 20 hostages.** A supermarket siege ends after 17 hours.

Footer: Thanksgiving Day — Trump spares the turkeys Freedom and Feathers.
